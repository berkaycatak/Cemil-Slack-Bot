/filltheblank-> Kullanici oyuna basladiginda "https://api.songiqapp.com/api/categories?language=en" istek atilacak sonra category list donecek. Buton olarak cikarilacak categoryler.
Oyun baslayacak. Her soru icin de "https://api.songiqapp.com/api/songs?category_id=en_pop&limit=1&withPreview=1" istek atilacak.
Sarkiyi cekicek ve python ile yazilan sey speech to text yapacak. These lyrics will be sende to grog then it will blank them for 
the user to fill in the input. It will give a score out of 100 and write the correct lyrics if the user gives the wrong answer.
